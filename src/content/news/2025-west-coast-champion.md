It's official — **Daniel Oliver is a champion.** He has taken the **Pro-division title** in the **West Coast Racing League's Super Formula Lights** series aboard the No. 125 — and he did it at the **first attempt**, wrapping up the crown in his very first season of competition.

Titles don't usually come this fast. Daniel's did because the approach behind it was built to travel: treat every session as a dataset, measure everything, and turn practice into a repeatable process rather than a hope. A championship on debut is the clearest proof yet that the method holds up under real pressure, against a full field, over a season — not just on a fast lap.

It's also the foundation the rest of the story is built on. The same racecraft and discipline that delivered the West Coast Racing League title are what carried Daniel from the simulator to an FIA international licence, a Praga campaign, and a 2026 IMSA debut. The championship isn't a footnote to that journey — it's where it started to become undeniable.
